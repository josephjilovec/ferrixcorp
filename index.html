About Ferrix

Rooted in Pennsylvania. Built for industry.

Ferrix Corp is a Pennsylvania-based industrial manufacturer organized around the requirements that matter most to customers, employees, and long-term capital partners: capable operations, disciplined execution, safety, quality, and sustained reinvestment.

Reliability is earned operationally.
